Why characters are not recoloured
An Open Peeps pose is two merged paths: one holds all the line art, the other covers the entire interior — neck, hands, forearms and ankles and part of the outfit, with no seam between them. Skin colour and clothing colour therefore cannot coexist on one figure. The interior is painted with the figure’s skin tone so anatomy always reads correctly, and only poses whose top falls in the line-art path are cast, so clothing reads dark. Painting the interior with the brand accent instead gives every figure brand-coloured hands, neck, wrists and ankles against a skin-toned face — that was a real defect, fixed 2026-08-25.
